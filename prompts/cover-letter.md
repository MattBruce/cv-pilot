# Tailored Cover Letter Prompt

Use this prompt to draft a compelling, evidence-backed cover letter or application pitch.

---

## Prompt Template

```markdown
You are an expert executive career strategist.

Draft a compelling, concise, and authentic cover letter for the role described below, using ONLY evidence from my Master CV.

### Inputs
1. Target Job Description:
[PASTE JOB DESCRIPTION HERE]

2. Master CV:
[PASTE OR REFERENCE master-cv.md HERE]

3. Target Constraints:
- Length: Max 350–400 words (3-4 paragraphs)
- Tone: Check **Section 13** (`Application Preferences & AI Instructions`) of `master-cv.md` for the user's personal tone preferences (typically conversational yet authoritative, direct, and human).
- Openers: Use natural, confident openers (e.g. "I would like to be considered for..." or leading straight into the technical hook). Never use stiff robotic boilerplate like "I am writing to apply for..." or "I am pleased to submit my application".
- Clichés: Strictly avoid corporate buzzword fluff ("thrilled to apply", "dynamic synergy", "rockstar developer", "passionate self-starter").
- Target Recipient: [Hiring Manager Name / Company Talent Team]

---

### Structure

1. **The Hook (Opening Paragraph):**
   - State the role being applied for and immediately connect my core identity to what the company is trying to achieve.

2. **The Evidence & Value Proposition (1–2 Paragraphs):**
   - Address the 2-3 most critical problems mentioned in the JD.
   - Use 2-3 concrete achievements from my Master CV with specific metrics, tools, and business outcomes as proof.

3. **Cultural & Mission Alignment:**
   - Briefly reflect why this specific team, product, or mission is appealing to me based on my career direction.

4. **The Close:**
   - Confident, polite call-to-action inviting a conversation.

---

### Guardrails
- **Strict Ground Truth:** Draw every metric, project, and outcome strictly from the Master CV. Do not invent details.
- **Verification List:** Flag any numbers or company-specific assumptions for me to check before sending.
```
